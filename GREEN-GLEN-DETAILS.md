## **🦅 Birdwatching Experience**

### **Featured Bird Species**
1. **Asian Paradise Flycatcher** (Terpsiphone paradisi)
   - White morph male with 30cm ribbon streamers
   - Summer breeding visitor (April - September)

2. **Kalij Pheasant** (Lophura leucomelanos)
   - Himalayan ground dweller with scarlet facial wattle
   - Resident species, often seen at dawn

3. **Slaty-headed Parakeet** (Psittacula himalayana)
   - Slate-grey hood with lime-green wings
   - Himalayan altitudinal migrant

4. **Red-billed Blue Magpie** (Urocissa erythroryncha)
   - 45cm graduated tail tipped with white
   - Resident forest icon

5. **Red Junglefowl** (Gallus gallus)
   - Wild ancestor of domestic poultry
   - Native ground bird

### **Birding Facilities**
- Forest veranda for natural birdwatching
- 5:30 AM hot ginger tea & packed breakfast boxes
- Binoculars & local field guides in lounge
- Tripod-friendly stone ledge
- Resident naturalist guided walks

### **Birding Trails**
1. **The Studio Point Trail** (1.8 km)
   - Famous for barbets, woodpeckers, minivets, and flycatchers

2. **Garud Tal & Hidimba Ridge** (2.4 km)
   - Tranquil lake basin with water birds and raptors

3. **Green Glen Garden & Forest Edge** (On-site)
   - Natural fruiting bushes and canopy perches

## **🎨 Website Design Features**

### **Modern Award-Winning Design Elements**

#### **1. Color Palette - Nature Inspired**
- **Forest Green**: #1A2E1C (primary dark)
- **Olive**: #4A6B45 (secondary)
- **Ivory**: #F9F7F4 (background)
- **Terracotta**: #C87456 (accent)
- **Sage**: #A8B89F (nature accent)
- **Moss**: #5F7A56 (deep green)

#### **2. Typography**
- **Display Font**: Cormorant Garamond (serif, elegant)
- **Body Font**: Inter (sans-serif, modern)
- **Sizes**: Responsive clamp() functions for fluid scaling
- **Line Heights**: 1.8-1.9 for excellent readability

#### **3. Modern Interactions**
- **Glassmorphism**: Frosted glass cards with backdrop blur
- **Organic Shapes**: Morphing background elements
- **Parallax Scrolling**: Smooth depth effects
- **Magnetic Hover**: Buttons lift on hover
- **Smooth Animations**: Cubic-bezier easing (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
- **Card Lifts**: -8px elevation on hover

#### **4. Spacing & Breathing Room**
- **Section Padding**: clamp(5rem, 12vw, 12rem)
- **Container Padding**: clamp(2rem, 7vw, 9rem)
- **Card Padding**: clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3.5rem)
- **Grid Gaps**: 8+ units (2rem+)
- **Max Width**: 1520px

#### **5. Visual Effects**
- **Smooth Scroll Reveals**: Fade up with stagger
- **Image Zoom**: 1.06x scale on hover
- **Gradient Overlays**: Subtle depth layers
- **Border Radius**: 12-24px for modern feel
- **Soft Shadows**: Organic multi-layer shadows
